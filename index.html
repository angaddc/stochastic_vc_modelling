Model: exit revenue and the exit multiple are lognormal (set by their P10 and P90) and can move together; failure is a weighted coin flip with a small recovery; each future round dilutes the stake. Payouts are discounted over each future's own holding period at the fair return. The full derivation, sources and three real-deal tests are in the methodology.

Presets for Figma, Navan and Artly use the sourced inputs from the methodology (Section 7). The page is a single self-contained file. Results match the R notebook to within simulation noise. Illustrative only, not investment advice.
